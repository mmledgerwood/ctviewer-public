What is this?

CT Scan Viewer shows CT scans in your browser: 2D slices in three planes, a 3D rendering, a freely-angled Plane Viewer, image adjustments, and measurement tools with a results table you can export. Your scan is converted on your own computer; nothing you load is uploaded.

Opening a scan

Choose or drop a folder
Drag a folder of DICOM files onto the upload area at the top of the left sidebar, or click Choose Files… and pick the folder. The conversion runs in your browser, so large studies depend on your computer's speed and on how fast the files can be read.
Slice thickness and Fast preview
Set these before choosing files. Slice thickness keeps every Nth slice to reach the thickness you enter (for example 0.9 mm on a 0.3 mm series keeps every third slice). Fast preview with no thickness entered keeps every second slice. Slices that aren't kept are never read, which is what makes loading faster — especially for folders that sync from a cloud drive. Averaging neighboring slices isn't available in the browser version.
Study, series, and patient
Series Info (top of the left sidebar) shows the patient name from the scan header, the study (the folder you picked), the series description, the number of slices loaded, and the voxel size. The top bar repeats the study and series.

Toolbar

The row of tools across the top of the window.

Interaction modes

Crosshair default

Click in any 2D panel to move the crosshair; it stays in sync across every view, the 3D rendering, and any pop-out window. In the 3D panel, a click picks a point on the surface and dragging orbits the view.

Pan / Zoom

Drag to move the image within a panel; scroll to zoom. Right-click-drag pans in any mode.

Measure

Opens the Measure panel on the right. Dragging or clicking in a 2D panel then draws the selected tool instead of moving the crosshair. See Measuring.

Rotate 3D

Drag in the 3D panel to orbit the volume. Rotation only happens while the mouse button is held.

Other buttons

Adjust closed by default

Opens Image Adjustments in the right sidebar: brightness, contrast, the intensity histogram, and the 3D clip slab. Opening Measure closes it, and vice versa.

Views

The layout menu. See Views.

Plane Viewer

Opens a freely-angled slice in its own window, starting from Axial, Sagittal, or Coronal. See Plane Viewer.

Undo / Redo

Reverses the last discrete change (adjustments, slab, view, pan, zoom, rotation). Redo re-applies it; any new change clears redo.

Reset view

Returns to the Large view with 3D Rendering, and resets pan, zoom, rotation, brightness, contrast, and the clip slab.

Views

Open the Views menu in the toolbar to choose a layout.

Large view 6

The default. One large view fills the left side, and the other three views stack in a column on the right: Axial, Sagittal, Coronal, or whichever aren't in the large view. The large view starts as 3D Rendering. To swap, drag a column view by its title bar onto the large view. A single click in a column view moves the crosshair there, as it does in the large view. Measuring works in the large view only.

4-Panel 5

Axial, Sagittal, Coronal, and 3D Rendering together. Each 2D panel's border and crosshair lines are colored by plane: axial red, coronal green, sagittal blue.

1-Panel 1–4

Choose one view to fill the window: Axial (1), Sagittal (2), Coronal (3), or 3D Rendering (4). Each entry in the 1-Panel submenu also has a small pop-out button that opens that view in its own window.

Orientation letters

Letters along the edges of the 2D panels are radiological orientation markers: S superior, I inferior, A anterior, P posterior, L left, R right. Axial shows A/P and L/R; Sagittal shows S/I and A/P; Coronal shows S/I and L/R.

Side panels

The right sidebar holds Image Adjustments or Measure, one at a time. Series Info is at the top of the left sidebar.

Image Adjustments

Brightness and contrast

Brightness moves the window level; contrast changes the window width. Reset returns each to its default.

Histogram

The intensity distribution of the volume. The two labels under it are the minimum and maximum in Hounsfield Units (HU).

3D Render — clip slab

Drag the two handles to cut away the bottom and top of the 3D rendering, so you can view just a slab of the volume.

Series Info and cursor

Series Info shows the patient name, study, series, slice count, and voxel size. The study name is the name of the folder you choose. Cursor shows the mm position, voxel index, and HU value under the crosshair, live.

Measuring

Click Measure in the toolbar. The Measure panel lists the tools below. Pick one, then draw in an Axial, Sagittal, or Coronal panel — or in the Plane Viewer. Every new measurement is added to the table automatically.

Line

Drag between two points. Reports the length in mm.

Rectangle, Ellipse, Circle

Drag to draw. Reports the area in mm². Circle keeps width and height equal.

Annotation

Drag to place an arrow, then type its label. The label sits at the tail of the arrow.

Freehand

Press and drag around the area you want. The enclosed area is reported in mm².

Spline

Click each point around the outline (at least three). Close it by clicking inside the yellow circle that marks the starting point, or by double-clicking. The area is reported in mm².

Angle

Click three points, with the vertex (the corner of the angle) in the middle. Reports the angle in degrees.

Working with measurements

Active Measurement box

While drawing, it shows the cursor position and a histogram of the pixels under the shape. Afterwards it shows the measurement you last drew, or the one you click. Esc cancels a shape in progress.

Right-click (2D panels)

Right-click a shape to Delete measurement (removes the shape and its table row) or Add to table.

Undo last and Clear all

These erase the drawings on the images only. Table rows stay. Loading a new scan starts a fresh table.

Table & export

Include Histograms

When checked, each new measurement's histogram statistics (mean, standard deviation, minimum, maximum) go on its table row, and the full histogram is stored for export. Right-click Add to table follows the same setting.

Table

Rows are numbered. Click a label cell to type a name. The × button removes that row only; the shape stays on the image.

Export CSV

Downloads the table: number, label, value, and histogram statistics where present.

Export Histograms

Downloads the full histogram of each row that was stored with Include Histograms: a label line, a row of voxel counts, and a row of HU values, one block per measurement.

Plane Viewer

A freely-angled slice through the volume, opened from the Plane Viewer button. It opens in its own window with the plane slice on the left and a 3D View on the right.

Controls

Tilt, Rotate, and Depth move the plane. The Plane/Object switch chooses whether the sliders move the plane through a fixed volume or move the volume past a fixed plane. The circular-arrow button resets the plane to where it opened.

Modes

Crosshair (default): a click moves the crosshair, dragging tilts and rotates the plane, and scrolling moves through the volume. Pan/Zoom: dragging pans and scrolling zooms. The yellow crosshair marker is shown in Crosshair mode and hidden in Pan/Zoom and Measure, so it doesn't get in the way.

Orientation

The plane image is mirrored left-to-right to match the main viewer, so a structure appears on the same side in both.

Measuring in the Plane Viewer

Turn on Measure in the main window, then use the Measure tools directly on the plane image. The cursor becomes a small dot while measuring. Results appear in the main window's table, and Undo last / Clear all work as they do elsewhere.

Pop-out windows

A pop-out is a separate browser window showing one view, opened from the Views menu's pop-out buttons or the Plane Viewer. The crosshair stays synchronized with the main window in both directions. You can drag a pop-out anywhere on screen, including a second monitor.
